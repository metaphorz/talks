Early Bookcases, Cupboards & Carousels

An interactive knowledge graph of how scholars stored & reached their books — built from the Lost Art Press post “Early Bookcases, Cupboards & Carousels” (28 Jul 2024). Artwork images via that post & their listed collections.

Click a node to view details. Drag to rearrange · scroll to zoom.

Select any node to explore.

Golden image nodes are the artworks from the post. Each links to the storage devices it depicts, plus the region, era, and people involved.
