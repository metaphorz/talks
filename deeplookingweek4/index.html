LAMESH

Six lenses for opening a subject
  • LLanguageWhat do we call it? → honeycomb; the Ainu upopoy — singing together
  • AArtHow might we re-see it? → an imagined reef, in video and song
  • MMathematicsWhat can we measure or model? → a state machine of building; a 3D model
  • EEngineeringHow is it made or structured? → the worms are masons: catch, place, cement
  • SScienceWhat processes explain it? → a reef that creates niches for life
  • HHumanitiesWhat does it mean to people? → kamuy: beings visiting from the spirit world

One object, six subjects — each lens opens a different field around it.

The Deep Looking App

An open, web-based gallery for looking through lenses

A web app (free on GitHub) that turns one image into many connections.

  1. 1Choose an image from a curated gallery.
  2. 2Hover over it — the image is divided into segments (sky, boardwalk, frond…).
  3. 3A window opens with a tab for each lens: poetry, geometry, art history…
  4. 4Pin it and explore — follow links, then go back to looking.
