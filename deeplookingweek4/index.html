Engineering

The worms are masons

They do not secrete a continuous mineral shell.

1

CATCH

Suspended sand grains and shell fragments.

2

PLACE

Particles are positioned with specialized building organs.

3

CEMENT

A rapid-setting protein bio-adhesive locks the structure underwater.

Engineering question: How can a structure be assembled, bonded, and maintained in moving seawater?

Masons, not shell-makers: catch, place, cement. Each step is an engineering problem in moving water.

Humanities

Meaning changes the object again
“

In Ainu cosmology, animals can be understood as kamuy — spirit-deities visiting the human realm. Stories and chants can place the nonhuman being at the center of the account, changing the relation between observer and observed.

A humanities lens asks not only “What is it?” but “What relationships and stories gather around it?”

The same object can be material, habitat, artifact, metaphor, memory, or gift.

Kamuy: the observed being at the centre of the story. Then the ring: six things the same stone can be.

The Deep Looking App

An open, web-based gallery for looking through lenses

A web app (free on GitHub) that turns one image into many connections.

  1. 1Choose an image from a curated gallery.
  2. 2Hover over it — the image is divided into segments (sky, boardwalk, frond…).
  3. 3A window opens with a tab for each lens: poetry, geometry, art history…
  4. 4Pin it and explore — follow links, then go back to looking.
